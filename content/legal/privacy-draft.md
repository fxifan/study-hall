## Who we are

Edumi is run by Feifan Wu, a sole trader in Victoria, Australia. Edumi has two student services:

- **Edumi** study groups, for students 13 and over: groups, a shared study timer, chat, friends and AI practice.
- **Edumi Study Hall**: supervised online study sessions that a parent books for their child.

Questions, privacy requests or complaints: feifanwork1@gmail.com. Edumi for Tutors has its own notice.

## What we collect

- **Account:** first name, last initial, email, year level, and birth month and year (only to check age). Parents: name and email.
- **Study groups:** your groups, chat and direct messages, your username and friends, study timer times, help requests, blocks and reports, and your privacy choices.
- **Practice:** the topics and notes you give us, what the AI makes, your answers, marks, points and rank.
- **Study Hall:** your child's subjects and school (optional), session goals, attendance, uploaded work, supervisor notes and messages.
- **Payments:** handled by Stripe. We keep your plan and Stripe IDs, never card details.
- **Usage:** page views (Vercel Analytics, no cookies) and a record of key actions, such as signing up or finishing a quiz, that never includes names or message text.

We don't collect photos, addresses, phone numbers or full dates of birth. We don't record video or audio, and we don't show ads.

## How we use it

To run Edumi: sign you in, show your groups and boards, mark quizzes, send login and account emails, take payments, keep students safe, and fix and improve the service. We don't sell your information.

## Who can see what

- **Group members** see your first name and last initial, your messages in that group, and (if you allow it) when you're studying and your minutes.
- **Friends** you accept can message you and see your study minutes on the Friends board, if you both switch it on. Friends are added by exact username only. Adults and under-18s can only become friends if they already share a group.
- **Your points, marks and practice** are private to you.
- **Parents** see their own child's Study Hall details and reports.
- **Supervisors** see what they need for the sessions they run.
- **Edumi administrators** can see all accounts and messages to review reports and keep students safe.

## Services we use

Supabase (database and sign-in, Sydney), Vercel (hosting, Sydney), Resend (emails), OpenAI (AI practice), Stripe (payments), and Zoom or Google Meet (Study Hall video). Some of these may process data outside Australia, including in the United States. OpenAI doesn't use our requests to train its models.

## Keeping and deleting

- Study Hall families: deleted automatically 7 days after the subscription ends.
- Students: email us to delete your account and everything in it. We'll do it within 30 days.
- Stripe keeps its own payment records as the law requires.

You can ask to see or correct your information at any time by emailing us.

## Child safety

- Under-13s can't sign up themselves. They can only use Study Hall, booked by a parent.
- Every supervisor holds a Working with Children Check that we've verified, and an adult is never alone on video with a student.
- Anyone can report a message or block another student. We review every report.
- To raise a concern, email feifanwork1@gmail.com. If someone is in immediate danger, call 000.

## Complaints and changes

If you're unhappy with how we handled your information, email us and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). We'll post changes to this notice here.
