## Who we are

Edumi (formerly Study Hall) is run by Feifan Wu, a sole trader. Edumi helps secondary students study with their friends (study groups, a shared study timer, group chat and AI practice), and Edumi Study Hall offers supervised online study sessions for school students in Victoria, Australia. Contact us at feifanwork1@gmail.com with questions, concerns or privacy requests.

## What we collect

- About you (the parent): your name, email address, the consents you give, and optionally how you heard about us.
- About your child: first name, last initial, year level, school if you choose, the subjects they take and any they'd like help with, an optional short note on current schoolwork (up to 200 characters), and the email address they use to log in. Please keep the note to schoolwork: no health or personal information.
- About each session: when your child joined and left, the tasks they planned and how they marked them (with any result or note they add), any photos or PDFs of their work they choose to upload, short factual check-in notes from the supervisor (for example "Phone visible once during the session"), help requests, and messages between your child and the supervisor.
- Uploaded work is optional. It's stored privately and can only be opened by your child, you, the staff of that session and Edumi administrators. Edumi doesn't mark it.
- Students who sign themselves up (13 and over): first name, last initial, year level, email address, and birth month and year (only to check they're 13 or older). Students under 13 can only join through a parent's account.
- Study groups: the groups a student creates or joins, their group chat messages, study timer sessions (start and stop times and the verified minutes credited), help requests, friend requests and blocks, and their privacy choices (for example whether they appear on boards). The timer records only that an Edumi study page was open and in use; it doesn't record which other apps, sites or documents are open.
- Practice: the topic and any notes a student pastes in to make notes, flashcards or a quiz, what the AI produces, their quiz answers and scores, points, and any content they flag as wrong. Points and scores are private to the student.
- Billing: Stripe processes payments. We store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs and the plan you chose, not your card details.
- When you agree to the house rules or this notice, we record the version, the time, and a one-way hash of your IP address.

## What we don't collect

- Edumi doesn't capture, record or store video, images or audio.
- We don't ask for a student's full date of birth, photo, address or phone number. Students who sign themselves up give a birth month and year only.
- We don't use advertising or third-party tracking on student pages.

## The video room

Sessions run on Zoom or Google Meet. A supervisor is present in the live room. Recording is turned off in Edumi's Zoom rooms, and Edumi doesn't record sessions. The video provider processes the call under its own privacy terms.

## Who can see what

- You can see your own children's details, attendance, goals and reports.
- A supervisor or tutor can see the roster, subjects, current-work notes, session plans, uploaded work and help requests for the sessions they're assigned to, so they can prepare and check in. On the tutoring plan, your child's help requests are shown to the tutor first.
- Edumi administrators can see everything, including all messages between supervisors and students. Supervisors can't delete messages.
- In Edumi study groups, members see each other's first name and last initial, group chat messages, group goal progress, and (if each member allows it) who is studying now and the group's board of study minutes. Accepted friends can see each other's total study minutes if both have the Friends board switched on. Nobody else sees a student's points, scores or practice.
- Students can report any message or block anyone. Edumi administrators review reports and can hide messages or pause a student's chat.
- A parent booking link for a Study Hall session shows the topic, time and price only, never chats, scores or boards.
- Outside study groups, students can't see other students' information.

## Where it's stored

Our database is hosted by Supabase in Sydney, Australia. The website is hosted by Vercel, with server code running in Sydney. Sign-in and account emails are sent through Supabase, our login provider. Notes, flashcards and quizzes are written by OpenAI's API from the topic and notes a student provides; OpenAI doesn't use API data to train its models, and we ask it not to store requests. Payments are processed by Stripe. The video room runs on Zoom or Google Meet.

## Keeping and deleting data

When your subscription ends, we keep your family's data for 7 days (in case you resubscribe), then delete it automatically: your account, your children's accounts and details, their session plans, attendance, reports, messages and uploaded work. Stripe keeps its own payment records, as the law requires.

A student who signed themselves up can ask us to delete their account the same way. You can also ask us to delete your child's data at any time by emailing feifanwork1@gmail.com, and we'll do it within 30 days.

## Child safety

- Every supervisor must hold a Working with Children Check that we have verified.
- An adult is never alone on video with a student. Help happens by text in the app, or in a breakout room with at least two students or a second adult. One-to-one tutoring (Study + Tutoring plan) happens in the main session room, with the supervisor and other students present.
- To raise a child-safety concern, email feifanwork1@gmail.com. The Edumi team reviews every concern and responds as soon as possible. If a child is in immediate danger, call 000.
