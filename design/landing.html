1.How it works

A real study room, run online.

It’s the after-school supervised study room, without the drive. One adult watches the room, everyone works, and you hear how it went.

  1. (a)

    You set it up

    Create a parent account, add your child, and agree to the house rules together. You pay weekly and can cancel any time.

  2. (b)

    They plan, then join

    From 3:50pm your child writes up to three goals for the session, then joins the video room with the camera on their hands and desk.

  3. (c)

    A tutor runs the room

    A proctor with a verified Working With Children Check supervises, keeps breaks on the timer, and helps when someone presses “I’m stuck”.

  4. (d)

    You hear how it went

    Every night: when they arrived and left, what they planned, what they finished, and a line from the proctor.

Plain and factual, written from what actually happened in the room. Good nights and bad nights look the same: just the facts.

  1. 1

4.House rules

Six rules. Same for everyone.

Your child agrees to these when you sign up, and sees them again before every session.

  1. 1Camera on, framed on your hands and desk. Face optional.
  2. 2Phone face-down inside the camera frame for the whole block.

5.Child safety

What we’ll never do.

These rules are built into the software itself, not just written in a policy.

  • 01

    Record your child

    Study Hall never captures, records or stores video, images or audio. The proctor watches the live room, and nothing is kept.

  • 02

    Allow private one-to-one video

    Help happens by text in the app, or in a breakout room with at least two students or a second adult. An adult is never alone on video with your child.

  • 03

    Use an unchecked adult

    Every proctor holds a Working With Children Check we’ve verified. If it’s unverified or expired, the system won’t let them run a room.

  • 04

    Delete the paper trail

    Every message between a proctor and a student is logged and visible to our admins. Proctors can’t delete them.

  • 05

    Collect more than we need

    First name, last initial, year level, and school if you choose. No birthday, photo, address or phone number. Stored in Sydney.

  • 06

    Go around you

    Only a parent can create a student account, after agreeing to the rules. Ask us to delete your child’s data any time and it’s done within 30 days.

No student-to-student messages, profiles, rankings or feeds. No ads or trackers on student pages. Privacy & child safety notice

7.Questions

Questions parents ask.

What does my child need?

A laptop or tablet with a camera, a reliable internet connection, and a desk. They join a Zoom or Google Meet room from the Join button on their Study Hall page.

Is this tutoring?

No. It’s a place to do the work, not a lesson. The proctor supervises and keeps the room on task. If your child is stuck, they press “I’m stuck” and the proctor helps by text in the app or in a small group breakout.

Who are the proctors?

Tutors who hold a Working With Children Check that we’ve verified. We track expiry dates, and no one can be put in charge of a room without a current check.

Is my child recorded?

No. Study Hall never captures, records or stores video, images or audio. The proctor watches the live room and that’s all.

Why hands and desk, not their face?

Because that’s where the work happens. Showing their face is optional. Phones go face-down inside the frame so the proctor can see they’re not in use.

What if they don’t turn up?

You’ll get a one-line message saying they didn’t attend, about 15 minutes after the session ends.

Do they have to come every night?

No. The plan covers every session from Monday to Thursday, so they can come as often as suits your family. Each report shows exactly when they were there.

Can I get reports on WhatsApp or WeChat?

Reports come by email. If you’d rather read them in WhatsApp or WeChat, tell us and we’ll send the same text there. You can also switch to one weekly summary.

How does billing work?

A$49 a week, charged weekly through Stripe. Change your card or cancel any time from your account. If the first week isn’t right for you, ask and we’ll refund it.

Can my child sign up on their own?

No. A parent creates every student account and agrees to the house rules and privacy notice first. Your child then gets an email invite to log in.
