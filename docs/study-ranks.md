# Study Ranks

13 ranks students climb with points (quiz marks, and 10 for finishing notes or flashcards; up to 60 a day). Brief: `edumi-ranks-claude-code.md` (7 Nov 2026). Leaderboard study minutes are separate and don't affect rank.

**Thresholds approved by the owner on 7 Nov 2026.** At the 60-point daily cap, Bronze takes a day, Gold about a week, Ruby about two weeks and Grandmaster about four months. Changing them means editing `RANKS` (the app passes thresholds to the database on every award) and, for students already ranked, deciding whether to re-initialise `student_ranks`. A unit test checks the migration's initial thresholds match the config.

Badges are original SVGs generated by `npx tsx scripts/rank-badges.ts` into `public/ranks/<id>.svg`, following the approved mockup's direction (shield, emblem, laurels growing with tier, gem from Ruby, horns for Diamond, crown for the top two). Simple shapes and thick strokes so they read at 16–20px. If an image fails, a coloured disc with the rank's initial stands in.

## How promotions work

- Every award (quiz submission, marking notes or flashcards done) inserts into `points_ledger`, then calls `sync_student_rank(profile, thresholds)` (`src/lib/social/rank-sync.ts`). Under a row lock it compares the committed balance with `student_ranks.tier`; if higher it updates the tier and inserts one `rank_promotions` row to the highest tier reached (several tiers = one event). `unique (profile_id, to_tier)` and the lock make retries and concurrent awards safe; ranks never go down.
- Existing students were initialised from their balances by the migration, with no promotion rows, so nobody is shown old ranks as new.
- A failed sync never fails the award; the next award re-syncs from the balance.

## Celebration

- Shown only for a recorded promotion of the signed-in student: right after quiz marks appear (never during submission), or on the next student page they open (not on the plan or checkout page, or mid-quiz). The new rank is already visible on the profile, ranks page and boards, celebration or not.
- Once only: tabs queue on a Web Lock per promotion; dismissing writes a local "seen" mark and acknowledges on the server, which also marks earlier unseen promotions seen. If several promotions piled up while away, one celebration shows the highest.
- Motion (Web Animations API, no new dependency, about 1 s): backdrop fades in (160 ms); the badge rises from 60% with a slight turn and settles with a small overshoot (650 ms); a soft ring in the tier colour spreads once behind it; "Rank up!" and the rank name slide up. Master Mind and Grandmaster Genius add a second ring and eight sparks and run a little longer (about 1.3 s). No flashing, looping or sound.
- Reduced motion: no animation; the dialog appears complete. Keyboard: focus goes to "Nice!", Tab stays in the dialog, Esc/close/backdrop dismiss, focus returns where it was. A status message announces "Rank up! You're now <rank>."

## Visibility

Students see their own rank and points (`/student/ranks`, Me). Other members of their groups and accepted friends see only the badge (group board, chat, Friends board), never points or scores. Students can hide their badge on the ranks page (`student_settings.show_rank`).

## References and approach

Reviewed for timing, clarity and reduced motion: Duolingo's design writing on milestone and achievement celebrations ([streak milestone design](https://blog.duolingo.com/streak-milestone-design-animation), [achievement badges](https://blog.duolingo.com/achievement-badges)), and general guidance on short celebratory micro-animations with reduced-motion fallbacks ([example](https://skillselion.com/skills/pablostanley/designteam-app/motion-designer)). Takeaways: one clear beat, about a second, tied to the badge's own identity, with a designed static fallback. Skill used: web-design-engineer (motion approach: native CSS/WAAPI before libraries; no emoji as icons).

## Tests

- Unit (`src/lib/social/social.test.ts`): 13 names in order, strictly increasing thresholds from 0, entry tier, just below / at thresholds, top tier, assets present, migration thresholds match.
- Database (`tests/rls/study-ranks.test.ts`, `npm run test:rls`): no promotion at Copper, just below vs at a threshold, 8 concurrent syncs → one promotion, multi-tier jump = one event, Grandmaster, no demotion, bad ladder rejected, signed-out callers refused.
- Browser (`tests/e2e/ranks.spec.ts`, dev server with `TUTOR_RESOURCES_OFFLINE=1`): rank-up after a quiz, Esc dismissal and focus, announcement, no repeat on refresh or in a second tab, another student never sees it, piled-up promotions → one celebration for the highest under reduced motion with no running animations, a retaken quiz adds no promotion.
